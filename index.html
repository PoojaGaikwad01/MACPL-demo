Two engineers, one steering column, and a habit of delivering early.

Ram Bhogale and Ajit Soundalgekar started MACPL in 1997 to make steering columns for scooters and three-wheelers. By 2000 the company was known for something rarer than capacity: developing a new part and its production line in record time.

Today we develop complex, safety-critical components to customer specification, from a single sub-assembly to complete assemblies, with in-house design, our own furnaces and SAP-run operations.
