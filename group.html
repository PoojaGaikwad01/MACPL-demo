MACPL belongs to the Applied Innovation & Technology Group, which shares HR, IT, finance and best practice across its engineering companies.

The AITG brand was developed around the group's intent, its market position, its relationships with all its stakeholders and its future business plans. It brings our companies one common culture, shared resources and uniform management processes.

One rising sun

Our identity shows a rising sun: a new beginning and an unlimited source of energy. It is designed to look global, contemporary, positive and impactful, and to portray superior, world-class technology.

Confident & Expert Blue

Pantone 287C. Blue stands for confidence and expertise.

Luminous & Determined Orange

Pantone 152C and five golden hues. The sun's six shaded sections stand for the group's six values.

Honest Business With Honest Relationships

The visionary

Keeping operational excellence and innovation in focus, the group's first company, Nirlep Appliances, started in 1968. Since then its companies have grown into consumer goods, aluminium rolling, specialty paints and coatings, dairy and chemical equipment, and auto components.

The group's guiding thought comes from its visionary, Neelkanth (Nanasaheb) Gopal Bhogale.
