About

Marathwada Auto Compo Pvt. Ltd. is where we develop complex, technically challenging auto components that contribute immensely to the safety of a vehicle. State-of-the-art equipment and well-controlled processes ensure process and product quality, the most crucial of all the factors.

MACPL started in 1997, when two technocrats, Ram Bhogale joined by Ajit Soundalgekar, set out to manufacture steering columns for scooters and three-wheel commercial and passenger vehicles. By 2000 the company was known for something rarer than capacity: developing a new part and its production line in record time.

Today we manufacture auto components required at large scale, from a single sub-assembly to complete assemblies, with in-house design, our own furnaces and SAP-run operations.
